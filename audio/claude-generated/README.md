These audio files were **generated by code written by Claude** (Anthropic's AI, used through Claude Code), based on our direction for the mood ("brighter, more space-like, radio beeps"). No samples, songs or sound libraries were used.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |

| `ambient-space-full.wav` | About 47 seconds of the same sound: a full loop of its four chords |
| `space-upbeat-30s.wav` | 30 seconds of upbeat retro space music for the demo video: Apollo's radio beep, bright chords (A – E – F#m – D), a sparkling arpeggio with echo, a beat that starts after a short build-up, a melody, and a final chord that fades out. Composed and rendered with code (Web Audio, rendered offline in the browser) |

## How the sound is made

The website doesn't play these files: it creates its sounds live in the browser with the **Web Audio API**, in [`js/app.js`](../../js/app.js):

- **Ambient sound** (section "Ambient sound"): slow bright chords (A major 7 → B/A → D major 7 → E), music-box chimes, radio beeps, a sonar ping and drifting wind. It fades out while the audiobook plays and has a mute button.
- **Intro sounds** (section "Intro: the first step on the Moon"): Apollo's radio "Quindar" beep, ladder clinks, the first step, footsteps, the flag going in and unrolling, a short 8-bit victory tune and a soft music bed.

The two files here are recordings of the ambient sound, captured from the website and saved as `.wav`. Because the chimes and beeps are random, every play sounds a little different.

The story narration in the [`audio/`](../) folder is **not** AI: it was recorded by Camila.
